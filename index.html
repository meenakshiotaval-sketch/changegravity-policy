Privacy Policy - Change Gravity: Puzzle Rooms

Overview

Change Gravity: Puzzle Rooms (the "App") is a puzzle game made by Otaval Zone ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you play the App. Last updated: 10 November 2026.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and the App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. We do not run our own servers and we do not use our own analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, stars, settings such as music/sound/vibration, selected ball skin, daily-challenge streak, Endless run and tutorial progress) is saved only in the App's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time from the App (Settings > Reset progress), by clearing the App's storage, or by uninstalling the App.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (for example model, operating system version and app version), and how you interact with ads. This data is collected and used by Google/AdMob under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your personal data from AdMob.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy - https://policies.google.com/privacy and "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" - https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites .

Some features let you watch an optional rewarded video ad to receive an in-game benefit (for example extra hints, undos or a continue). Watching these ads is always your choice.

Your choices about ads and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region where it is required, the App shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > "Privacy options (ads)" when that option is shown. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, wording differs by device).

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone other than the advertising service described above, which processes data itself.

Security and retention

Because progress data stays on your device, its security depends on your device's security. We keep no copy of it. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at the same link, with the "Last updated" date changed.
